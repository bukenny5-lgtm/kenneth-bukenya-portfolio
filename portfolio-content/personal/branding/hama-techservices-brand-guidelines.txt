HAMA TECHSERVICES BRAND GUIDELINES

Primary portfolio brand:
Kenneth Bukenya

Professional identity:
Full-Stack Developer & Business Systems Engineer

Business and service brand:
Hama Techservices

Logo usage:
- Use the horizontal logo in wide layouts.
- Use the stacked logo in square or narrow layouts.
- Use the icon alone for favicons and social avatars.
- Use the dark-background logo in footers and dark sections.
- Maintain clear space around the logo.
- Do not stretch, rotate or recolour the logo.
- Do not place the logo over visually busy photographs.

Brand character:
Professional, reliable, practical, modern and business-focused.

Typography recommendation:
- Headings: Manrope or Inter
- Body text: Inter
- Technical labels: IBM Plex Mono

Accessibility:
- Use navy text on white or light backgrounds.
- Use white text on navy backgrounds.
- Do not use teal for long paragraphs.
- Maintain strong contrast for buttons and links.
